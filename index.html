01

The Problem

Why color-invariant recognition?

Saree designs can appear visually different when their colors, lighting conditions, or palettes change. A recognition system that relies heavily on color may therefore confuse visually similar designs.

This project focuses on learning visual representations that emphasize the underlying design structure rather than depending primarily on color information.

🎯

Objective

Retrieve the correct saree design even when the query image has a substantially different color palette.
